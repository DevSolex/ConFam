What we do

Sellers already close deals in WhatsApp every day. ConFam does the part that usually hurts: turning a sold item into a payment link, watching for the money, and confirming it without asking for a screenshot.

Everything routes to the seller's own verified bank account. There is no ConFam balance and no custody step anywhere in the flow.

  • REGISTER, ONBOARD and PAY all happen inside the chat you already sell in.
  • Every sale gets its own one-time payment link — a paid link can't be reused.
  • Buyers pay by card, bank transfer, or bank/OPay through Paystack.
  • Payments settle straight to your verified account; ConFam never holds funds.
  • Confirmation arrives by WhatsApp, and duplicate payments never double-count.

Why we built it

A transfer screenshot asks a seller to trust a picture. A real payment and an edited one can look identical, so she either ships the goods and hopes, or she makes a genuine customer wait while she checks.

So we made the payment confirm itself. Money is verified against Paystack's event record — the rail's own word, never a picture — and settles to the seller's own verified account. There is no ConFam balance where money stops on the way there.

Why sellers can trust it

Three guarantees that hold whether you take one order a week or a hundred a day.

ConFam never holds your money

Every payment routes to your own verified bank account. There is no intermediate ConFam balance for a payment to sit in.

A duplicate confirmation counts once

Confirmations are idempotent on the payment provider's event id. If a webhook fires twice, your ledger still shows one sale.

The ledger only moves forward

Amounts are stored as whole-number kobo in an append-only ledger. Nothing can be rewritten after the fact.

How it works

Nothing to install and nothing new to learn. You keep selling the way you already sell.

  1. Type PAY in the chat

    Text PAY 2500 Jordan to the ConFam account. You get back a payment link — one per sale — right away.

  2. Buyer pays their way

    The buyer opens the link and pays by card, bank transfer, or bank/OPay on Paystack's page. The price and the destination account are fixed by the link, not by anything they type.

  3. Both of you get confirmation

    The payment confirms against Paystack's record. You get a WhatsApp "Payment received", the money settles to your verified bank account, and the sale becomes one kobo record in the ledger. No screenshot, no double count.
